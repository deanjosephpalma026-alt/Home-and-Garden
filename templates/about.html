About Home And Garden

Your trusted partner in creating beautiful and comfortable living spaces

Our Story

Home And Garden was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to bring comfort and beauty into every home through carefully curated home and garden essentials. We believe that your living space should be a reflection of your personality and a sanctuary where you can relax, create, and thrive.

What started as a passion project has grown into a trusted e-commerce platform connecting customers with quality products from verified sellers across the Philippines. We've built a community of home enthusiasts, gardeners, and lifestyle seekers who share our commitment to quality and style.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make premium home and garden products accessible to everyone. We strive to provide a seamless shopping experience while supporting local sellers and artisans. Through our platform, we aim to:

  • Offer a diverse selection of high-quality home and garden products
  • Support local sellers and small businesses in growing their reach
  • Provide exceptional customer service and reliable delivery
  • Create a community of home and garden enthusiasts
  • Make home improvement accessible and enjoyable for everyone
